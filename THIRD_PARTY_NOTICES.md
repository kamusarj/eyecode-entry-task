# Third-party sources and artifacts

This submission includes original experiment code plus vendor/research sources. Existing licenses and attribution files remain with their respective source trees. No blanket license is applied to those materials.

- ROSOrin Pro simulation/model assets: Hiwonder vendor package; source metadata is retained under `task1_rosorin/src/`. Tutorial: https://wiki.hiwonder.com/projects/rosorin-pro/en/latest/docs/9_Gazebo_Simulation.html.
- HoMMI: https://github.com/xxm19/hommi, with its MIT license; UMI and RB-Y1 WBC dependency notices are retained under `task2_mobile_manipulation/third_party/hommi/deps/`.
- MuJoCo Menagerie Panda: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie; licenses are included with the source and model assets.
- Demonstrations and derived review samples: https://huggingface.co/datasets/real-stanford/hommi. Bundled samples, plan and look-at values derive from the Laundry-v3 subset and retain the dataset's applicable terms.
- DINOv3: https://huggingface.co/facebook/dinov3-vits16-pretrain-lvd1689m. The HoMMI checkpoint was trained from this backbone; reuse is subject to its model license/access terms. The original gated pretrained model cache and credentials are not included.

Pinned source/model revisions are in `task2_mobile_manipulation/configs/third_party_versions.json`. The only substantive local modification to the official HoMMI source is the documented stream-decoding patch; lightweight dependency shims and the experiment harness live outside its source tree. Generated bytecode and Git histories are excluded.
